About me

Hi, I’m David

PhD Student | Electrical Engineering, Carleton University

I’m a PhD student in Electrical Engineering (EE) at Carleton University in Ottawa, Canada. My research is in applied electromagnetics, specifically antennas and metasurfaces for current and next-generation communication systems. Much of my work focuses on the mmWave and sub-THz regime for beamforming technologies, like leaky-wave antennas and reflectarrays.

I completed my undergraduate degree (BASc) in EE at the University of Waterloo (2018–2023) and a Master’s degree (MASc) at Carleton University (2023–2025), where my thesis was on optically-transparent antennas and metasurfaces for 5G networks. My complete CV can be found here, and a list of my publications here.

In my free time, I enjoy photography and have been learning about this art for the past 4–5 years. I have photo galleries for several of my trips throughout Canada and in other countries, such as Japan and Colombia. My photography portfolio can be viewed here.
